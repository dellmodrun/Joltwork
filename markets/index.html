WHAT THE LABELS MEAN

Launch provenance is separate from market activity.

A market can be live without being launched through Joltwork. Joltwork therefore never infers provenance from a token name, ticker or trading venue.

Joltwork launch means the interface has a confirmed launch receipt containing the Mint and Solana transaction signature. A device receipt proves what this browser observed; a published registry entry is suitable for cross-device discovery.

Live market means a third-party indexer has observed a liquidity pool and trading data for the Mint. Price and volume are informational observations, not guarantees, endorsements or investment advice.
