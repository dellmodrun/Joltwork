FAQ / LAUNCH

Before creating a coin.

Does a default policy prevent users from adding stricter limits?

No. The issuer policy is a baseline. A trader may bind tighter max-input, min-output, expiry or route constraints to an individual transaction.

Is the policy tied to one liquidity pool?

No. Joltwork’s intent policy is evaluated around the chosen transaction route, allowing it to move across supported launchpads, routers and markets.

Who signs the launch transaction?

The connected creator wallet. Joltwork compiles the plan and exposes the exact output; it does not take custody of the creator’s signing authority.

Need the execution model first?

Review Jolt Guard’s pre-hook, CPI route and postcondition phases.
