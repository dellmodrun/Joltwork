THE CONCEPT

A settlement hook that follows the trade—not the pool.

Pool hooks are powerful, but they only govern activity inside one pool. Joltwork binds policy to a user’s transaction intent, allowing the same max-cost, minimum-output, route and counterparty rules to travel across launchpads, routers and markets.

The transaction commits only when the realized outcome matches the signed economic intent.
