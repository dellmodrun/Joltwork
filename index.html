THE CONCEPT

A settlement hook that follows the trade—not the pool.

Pool hooks are powerful, but they only govern activity inside one pool. Joltwork binds policy to a user’s transaction intent, allowing the same max-cost, minimum-output, route and counterparty rules to travel across launchpads, routers and markets.

The transaction commits only when the realized outcome matches the signed economic intent.

EXECUTION / LIFECYCLE

From declared outcome to atomic settlement.

Joltwork wraps an existing route. It does not replace market liquidity; it makes the user’s economic constraints executable.

STEP / PREPARE

Declare

The wallet defines maximum input, minimum output, expiry, route and optional policy data.

STEP / AUTHORIZE

Sign

A canonical manifest produces a deterministic intent identity bound to the authority.

STEP / EXECUTE

Route

Jolt Guard snapshots balances and invokes the selected launchpad, router or market through CPI.

STEP / SETTLE

Verify

Balances are reloaded. The transaction commits with a receipt or reverts as one atomic unit.

WHY JOLTWORK

Portable guarantees across fragmented liquidity.

TRANSACTION SCOPERoute-independent

SETTLEMENT MODELAll or nothing
