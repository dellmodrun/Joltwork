on the gap

a swap can be correctly quoted and still be incompletely explained. matching the quoted output proves delivery. it does not prove that every deduction was disclosed.

the study behind Joltwork examined 7,873 hook deployments, 50,011 pools and 296 million events. it found charging capability in 24.30% of registered hooks and deductions in 59.23% of accounted swaps involving charging-capable hooks. recorded revenue across priced hooks reached $3.97 million.

these are ethereum and bnb smart chain measurements, not solana statistics. Joltwork brings the disclosure question—not the dataset—to solana.

on the translation

solana does not reproduce the uniswap v4 pool manager. a swap is a transaction whose instructions can invoke routers, pools, token programs and custom programs through cross-program invocation. the evidence sits in the message, address lookup tables, inner instructions, logs, balances and mint state.

token-2022 transfer fees can retain value during a transfer, while transfer hooks can invoke a configured program whenever a mint moves. neither mechanism is inherently abusive. the disclosure problem begins when its economic effect is absent from what the trader is shown.

on the evidence

Joltwork is not a black-box safety score. every field must point to a transaction message, CPI path, pre/post balance, token-2022 extension or slot-bound program state. explained value is itemized. ambiguous value is marked unresolved.

the output is a compact receipt: what the wallet authorized, what it received, which programs moved value, which fees were declared, and which deltas were absent from the interface breakdown.

on the asset

Joltwork is intended to launch publicly on pump.fun and become the first market observed under the Joltwork model. its trades form a live solana dataset: quotes, routes, program invocations, balance changes and disclosure gaps can be studied from the beginning of the market.

the token is not equity, a revenue claim or a promise of yield. it is the public coordination object around an open research experiment: can a market make its own execution legible while it is being formed?

on the product

Joltwork is being built into a production-grade settlement intelligence stack for solana. the accounting engine is packaged for wallets, dex interfaces, explorers and institutional data systems. every integration receives the same normalized answer: what the wallet authorized, what each program moved, what the trader received, and which deltas remain unresolved.

the commercial standard is deterministic decoding, reproducible accounting, slot-aware program fingerprints, explicit confidence, stable schemas and low-latency delivery.

on the standard

Joltwork is moving step by step toward a complete commercial product. coverage grows program by program, while supported evidence is attributed, incomplete evidence is labeled, and each receipt is reproducible from its signature and slot.

every unit paid, received, retained, withheld, burned or redirected should be reconcilable with the explanation shown to the trader.

development note

Joltwork is being built as a complete settlement-intelligence stack: deterministic transaction decoding, a slot-aware program registry, balance-ledger reconciliation, token-2022 accounting and a receipt api designed for direct product integration.

the Joltwork market becomes the first continuous production dataset. live routes expand decoder coverage, validate simulation-to-confirmation drift and harden attribution rules from the launch block forward.
