ABSTRACT / PRODUCT THESIS

Launch coins with rules.
Trade with guarantees.

Joltwork is a programmable settlement layer for Solana. A coin issuer can publish a default economic policy; a trader can bind stricter constraints to one transaction. Jolt Guard executes the selected route through CPI and permits settlement only when the realized token balances satisfy the signed intent.

PRODUCT / 01

Launch Coin

Create a Token-2022 coin and compile the default policy that wallets and interfaces can present to traders. Pump create_v2 already creates Token-2022 mints; Joltwork adds an intent policy above the trading route without requiring the coin to belong to one pool.

PRODUCT / 02

Trade with Intent

Bind economic limits to one route. Jolt Guard snapshots the source and destination accounts, invokes the route, then atomically rejects settlement if input, output, expiry or a policy hook fails.

RESEARCH / ORIGIN

      
From disclosure research to enforceable settlement

      
The research question is simple: when an interface promises one economic result and execution produces another, can the difference be measured and explained? Joltwork moves that question from post-trade observation to pre-trade enforcement on Solana.
