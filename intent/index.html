FAQ / INTENTS

Execution questions.

Does Joltwork provide the swap quote?

No. A router or market supplies the executable accounts, data and quote. Joltwork canonicalizes the user’s accepted limits around that route. A production adapter must translate the router response into the ordered CPI account set; the current browser tool compiles the intent, not the final transaction.

Can one intent cross multiple programs?

Yes, when those instructions are represented by the ordered route-account set passed to the wrapper and each invoked program is permitted by the selected policy.

What is the JRC1 receipt?

A compact return payload containing the intent identity and realized balance deltas, designed for wallets and interfaces to consume after successful execution.
